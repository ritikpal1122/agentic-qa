## Description

As a PayLite user, I want to send money from my wallet to a saved contact, so that I can pay friends quickly.

Details are in the linked docs:
- PRD: [Send Money PRD](../prd/send-money.md)
- Design: [Send Money screens](../design/send-money.md)
- Tech: see the attached RFC

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** User can pick a saved contact, enter an amount and send.
- **AC-2** A fee of **1.5%** of the amount is shown on the confirmation screen before sending.
- **AC-3** If the wallet balance is not enough, show "Insufficient balance" and do not send.
- **AC-4** If the transfer would go over the daily limit, show "Daily limit exceeded" and do not send.
- **AC-5** After a successful send, show a success screen with a transaction reference.
